# Identity proposal

Screenshots of `proposal.html`, a standalone page that applies the tokens and
control kinds of research R3 and R4 to the results and criterion states, with
lines from the Turkish evaluation recording. Rendered in headless Chromium at
880×600 and 1100×760, light and dark. The first candidate is the real search
result; the other two rows are illustrative.

The page loads the Atkinson Hyperlegible woff2 files from beside it; they are
not committed here (they ship through Fontsource once the app adopts them). To
re-render, copy the four `files/*-wght-normal.woff2` from
`@fontsource-variable/atkinson-hyperlegible-next` and `…-mono` next to it.
